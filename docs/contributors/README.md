# Student roster and individual contribution reports

**Issues and PRs are the ongoing contribution record.** Keep ownership, evidence,
review, and progress in your Individual tasks. No separate portfolio table or
weekly contribution log is required. This directory holds individual reports.

## First contribution

1. Follow the [student guide](../student-start.md) to fork/clone and open an
   Individual onboarding task under Group goal #5.
2. Add one alphabetical row to [CONTRIBUTORS.md](../../CONTRIBUTORS.md), containing
   your preferred public name and GitHub profile link. Your username is sufficient.
   Remove the empty-roster notice if you are the first student to join.
3. Open an introduction PR with that roster change. Use `Closes #YOUR_TASK_NUMBER`
   and `Relates to #5`, substituting your actual task number. No personal profile
   file, research result, or report is required for onboarding.

An existing introduction PR or profile remains valid evidence; do not resubmit or
delete earlier work. Keep private emails, student IDs, and grades out of the roster.

## Record work once

In your own [Individual task](../issue-workflow.md), state your deliverable and
agreed assessment stage, link evidence/PRs, identify your part and collaborators,
and post the existing short weekly evidence/next-step/blocker update. Link useful
review comments there when relevant. No second table or profile update is needed.

Research notes, critique, reproduction attempts, design, code, analysis, writing,
review, and presentation work all count when supported by evidence. Describe
planned work as planned; rigorous negative findings and rejected proposals can
be useful contributions. Do not rank students by issues, commits, or activity counts.

## Individual contribution report

Each student opens their own report task under [Group goal #23](https://github.com/zhongnz/Fall26VIP_Agentic_Risk/issues/23).
Working targets are **draft November 6** and **final November 20, 2026**. At the
draft checkpoint, copy the [report template](_individual-report-template.md) to
`docs/contributors/YOUR-GITHUB-USERNAME.md`. If you already have a file there,
preserve its existing content and add the report sections. Existing portfolio
tables may remain as historical evidence; they need no further routine updates.

Explain your responsibilities, decisions, evidence, effect on the group study,
collaboration/presentation roles, limitations, and learning. Link directly to
your Individual tasks, PRs, reviews, and artifacts instead of copying activity logs.
Distinguish your work from collaborators' work. About 1–2 pages plus links is guidance.

The [tentative grading scheme](../grading-proposal.md) keeps the individual report
at **10%**, assessing attribution and explanation separately from work quality.
Submit the report through a PR linked to your report task; the instructor confirms
any official course submission route. Keep scores and private feedback outside GitHub.

## Final group credit and corrections

The [shared report](../reports/README.md) retains a short contribution summary:
who did what, with evidence links reviewed by the people involved. Plain role
descriptions are enough; CRediT labels are optional for a later publication.
See the [credit and authorship guidance](../cohort-guide.md#credit-and-authorship).

Correct attribution in the original task, PR, or report where it appears, with
supporting evidence and collaborator review. Preserve original commit authors;
[CONTRIBUTING.md](../../CONTRIBUTING.md) explains Git attribution and review.
